Installation Tips

  • Add 10% extra tiles for cuts and breakage.
  • Measure outlet and window areas separately.
  • Smaller tiles may require more grout lines.
  • Glossy tiles reflect more kitchen lighting.

FAQ

How many extra tiles should I buy?

Most installers recommend purchasing 10% extra material for cuts and replacements.

Can this calculator be used for bathroom walls?

Yes, it works for kitchen backsplashes, bathroom walls, and decorative tile areas.
