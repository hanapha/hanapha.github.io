Installation Tips

  • Add 10% extra tiles for cuts and waste.
  • Pattern layouts may require additional material.
  • Measure outlets and fixtures separately.
  • Use waterproof grout in wet areas.

FAQ

How many backsplash tiles do I need?

The required number depends on wall size, tile dimensions, and installation pattern.

Should I buy extra tiles?

Yes, extra tiles help cover cutting waste, repairs, and future replacements.
