Installation Tips

  • Add extra planks for cutting waste and repairs.

  • Acclimate wood planks before installation.
  • Measure room dimensions carefully for accuracy.

FAQ

How many extra planks should I buy?

It is recommended to buy 5–10% extra to cover installation waste and future repairs.

Can this calculator be used for wall panels?

Yes, the calculator works for flooring, wall cladding, decking, and wood panel projects.
