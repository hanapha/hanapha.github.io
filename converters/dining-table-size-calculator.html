FAQ

How much space should be around a dining table?

Ideally, leave at least 90 cm of clearance around the table for comfortable movement and chair spacing.

What shape dining table is best?

Round tables work well in small spaces, while rectangular tables are ideal for larger dining rooms and bigger families.
