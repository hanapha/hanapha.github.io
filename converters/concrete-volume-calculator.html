Concrete Tips

  • Add extra concrete for uneven surfaces and waste.
  • Thicker slabs are needed for heavy vehicle loads.
  • Always prepare the base surface before pouring concrete.
  • Use reinforcement for structural durability.

FAQ

How much extra concrete should I order?

Most contractors recommend ordering 5–10% extra concrete to avoid shortages.

What thickness is recommended for concrete slabs?

Residential slabs commonly use thicknesses between 10–15 cm depending on usage.
