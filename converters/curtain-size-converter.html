Curtain Measurement Tips

  • Use wider curtains for a fuller appearance.
  • Hang curtains higher to make ceilings look taller.
  • Floor-length curtains create a luxurious look.
  • Add extra width for pleated curtain styles.

FAQ

How wide should curtains be?

Curtains are usually 1.5–2 times wider than the window width for better fullness.

Should curtains touch the floor?

Floor-length curtains are recommended for most modern and elegant interiors.
