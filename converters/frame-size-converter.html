Frame Design Tips

  • Gallery frames usually include extra white border (matting).
  • Large frames create stronger visual impact on walls.
  • Minimal frames work well for modern interiors.
  • Always consider wall space before choosing frame size.

FAQ

What is a gallery frame?

A gallery frame includes extra border space (matting) to highlight the artwork or photo.

Should frame size match image size?

Frames are usually slightly larger than the image to allow for borders and mounting space.
