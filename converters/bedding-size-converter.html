Bedding Selection Tips

  • Always check mattress thickness before buying sheets.
  • King-size bedding provides extra comfort for couples.
  • Duvets should be larger than mattress dimensions.
  • Different countries may use different size standards.

FAQ

Are bedding sizes universal?

No, bedding dimensions vary between countries and manufacturers.

Should duvet covers be larger than the bed?

Yes, slightly larger duvet covers provide better coverage and comfort.
