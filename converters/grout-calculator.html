Grouting Tips

  • Smaller grout joints require less grout.
  • Textured tiles may consume more grout.
  • Always buy extra grout for repairs.
  • Choose grout color that complements tile style.

FAQ

How much extra grout should I buy?

Most installers recommend buying 10–15% extra grout for waste and future maintenance.

Does tile size affect grout usage?

Yes, smaller tiles usually require more grout because they create more grout lines.
