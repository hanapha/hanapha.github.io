FAQ

What is the ideal TV viewing distance?

The ideal viewing distance depends on your TV size and screen resolution. Sitting too close or too far may reduce viewing comfort.

Why is viewing distance important?

Proper distance helps improve picture quality, reduce eye strain, and create a better entertainment experience.
