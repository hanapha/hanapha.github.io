Paint Coverage Tips

  • 1 liter of paint typically covers 10 m².
  • Dark colors may require additional coats.
  • Always prepare extra paint for touch-ups.
  • Primer may improve paint coverage.

FAQ

How much paint do I need for a room?

The amount depends on wall size, paint coverage, and the number of coats applied.

Should I apply two coats of paint?

Yes, two coats generally provide better color consistency and durability.
