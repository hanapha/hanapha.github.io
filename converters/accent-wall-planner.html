Accent Wall Tips

  • Choose the wall that naturally draws attention first.
  • Use darker tones for dramatic focal points.
  • Wood panels and textured paint add depth.
  • Keep surrounding walls neutral for balance.

FAQ

What is an accent wall?

An accent wall is a single wall designed with a different color, texture, or material to create a focal point.

Which wall should become an accent wall?

Usually the first visible wall, TV wall, bed headboard wall, or fireplace wall works best as an accent wall.
