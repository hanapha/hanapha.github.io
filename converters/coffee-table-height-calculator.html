Coffee Table Guidelines

  • Coffee tables should generally match sofa seat height.
  • Leave 40–45 cm space between sofa and coffee table.
  • Low-profile tables work well in modern interiors.
  • Traditional living rooms often use taller tables.

FAQ

What is the ideal coffee table height?

Most coffee tables are between 40–45 cm high, depending on sofa height and room style.

Should a coffee table be lower than the sofa?

Yes, a coffee table is usually the same height or slightly lower than the sofa seat.
