Mirror Placement Tips

  • Mirrors can make rooms feel larger and brighter.
  • Bathroom mirrors are usually slightly narrower than the vanity.
  • Round mirrors work well in minimalist interiors.
  • Large mirrors create stronger visual impact.

FAQ

How wide should a mirror be?

Mirrors are commonly 70–90% of the furniture width below them.

Should mirrors match furniture width?

Full-width mirrors can create a modern luxury appearance, while smaller mirrors provide balanced proportions.
