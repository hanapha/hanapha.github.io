Flooring Tips

  • Add 5–10% extra material for cutting waste.
  • Diagonal patterns may require more flooring.
  • Measure every room carefully before ordering.
  • Different flooring materials have unique durability levels.

FAQ

How much extra flooring should I buy?

Most professionals recommend purchasing 5–10% additional material for cuts and mistakes.

Which flooring type is best for homes?

Vinyl, laminate, and hardwood are popular choices depending on budget and durability needs.
