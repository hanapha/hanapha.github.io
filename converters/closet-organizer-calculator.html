Closet Organization Tips

  • Leave at least 90 cm hanging height for shirts.
  • Use drawers for accessories and folded clothes.
  • Keep shelves between 30–40 cm apart.
  • Use upper shelves for seasonal storage.

FAQ

What is the ideal closet depth?

Most closets are around 55–65 cm deep for comfortable clothing storage.

How many shelves should a closet have?

The ideal number depends on storage needs, but 4–6 shelves work well for most closets.
