**DIY Over-the-Toilet Bathroom Storage Shelf**

### Using Vertical Space Efficiently

Bathrooms often have limited storage, especially in smaller homes or apartments. The wall space above the toilet is frequently underused and can be transformed into valuable storage with a custom shelf. An over-the-toilet shelf can hold towels, toiletries, and decorative accents. DIY shelving offers affordable and customizable solutions. Vertical storage can dramatically improve organization.

Custom shelves can be designed to match bathroom décor and available dimensions. Open shelving keeps frequently used items easy to access. Decorative baskets can improve organization even further. Functional design can make bathrooms feel more spacious and tidy. Small upgrades can create significant improvements.

### Planning and Measuring

Measure the width and height above the toilet carefully. Consider how much clearance is needed for comfort and accessibility. Decide whether you want one shelf or multiple stacked levels. Sketch the design before gathering materials. Good planning helps prevent installation issues.

Select moisture-resistant wood or sealed materials suitable for humid environments. Decorative brackets can enhance style and support. Matching finishes can create a cohesive appearance. Thoughtful material choices improve durability. Preparation supports smoother construction.

### Building and Mounting

Cut shelf boards to the desired dimensions and sand all edges thoroughly. Apply paint or sealant before installation for easier finishing. Mount brackets securely into wall studs whenever possible. Use a level to ensure perfectly straight shelves. Secure installation is essential for safety.

Test the shelves with light items before adding heavier supplies. Reinforce supports if needed. Strong construction improves reliability over time. Stable shelves help prevent accidents. Attention to detail matters.

### Organizing and Styling

Use baskets or jars to organize toiletries neatly. Add decorative candles or plants for visual appeal. Avoid overcrowding to maintain a clean and open look. Thoughtful styling can make bathrooms feel more relaxing. Practical storage improves daily convenience.

Clean shelves regularly to prevent dust and moisture buildup. DIY bathroom projects can provide quick and affordable upgrades. Personalized storage makes small spaces more functional. Smart design supports better organization. Simple improvements can greatly enhance comfort.

- **Measure above the toilet carefully.**
- *Use moisture-resistant finishes* for durability.
- **Mount into studs** when possible.
