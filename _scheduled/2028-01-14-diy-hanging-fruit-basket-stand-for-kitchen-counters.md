**DIY Hanging Fruit Basket Stand for Kitchen Counters**

### Planning the Stand

Measure the available countertop or hanging area before selecting dimensions. Decide how many basket levels you need based on household produce habits. Sketch the design to visualize balance and accessibility. Accurate planning improves functionality and appearance. Preparation helps prevent mistakes.

### Building and Assembly
