### Planning the Rack

Measure the available hanging space and determine how many herb bundles you want to dry. Choose a design with enough spacing for airflow. Sketch the rack layout, including hooks or hanging rods. Accurate planning improves usability and appearance. Preparation helps avoid construction issues.

Select lightweight but durable wood or metal materials. Consider food-safe finishes if herbs may contact surfaces directly. Matching finishes can coordinate with kitchen décor. Material choices affect both durability and style. Good preparation supports better craftsmanship.

### Building and Assembly

Cut the frame components to size and sand all surfaces thoroughly. Assemble the rack securely and install hanging hooks evenly. Ensure adequate spacing for herb bundles to dry properly. Strong construction improves reliability. Precision enhances visual appeal.

Hang fresh herbs carefully and monitor drying progress regularly. Functional storage can preserve garden harvests beautifully. Thoughtful finishing enhances satisfaction. Quality workmanship matters.
