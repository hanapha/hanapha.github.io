### Planning the Station

Measure your pet's bowl size and determine the ideal feeding height. Decide how much storage space you need for food and accessories. Sketch the station layout, including bowl cutouts and storage compartments. Accurate planning improves usability and appearance. Preparation helps prevent design mistakes.

### Building and Assembly

Cut all station components to size and sand edges thoroughly. Assemble the frame securely and cut precise openings for food and water bowls. Add storage compartments or drawers as needed. Strong construction improves reliability and safety. Precision enhances function and appearance.

Test the station with your pet before regular use. Adjust placement for comfort and accessibility. Functional furniture can improve feeding routines significantly. Thoughtful finishing enhances satisfaction. Quality workmanship matters.
