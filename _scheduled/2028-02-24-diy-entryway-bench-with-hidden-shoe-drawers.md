### Planning the Bench

Measure the available entryway space carefully, including seating height and walking clearance. Determine how many drawers you need based on household storage requirements. Sketch the design to visualize proportions and functionality. Accurate planning improves usability and appearance. Preparation helps avoid costly mistakes.
